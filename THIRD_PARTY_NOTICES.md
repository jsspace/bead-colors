Artkal M Mini RGB values were transcribed from Artkal's 2025 M Mini RGB Color Chart. Perler product codes and names were checked against Perler's Shop by Color catalog. The 23 PER176xx RGB references were sampled from Perler product swatch photographs in September 2026. These numbers are screen references, not measurements of physical beads.

MARD set membership and special-effect classifications follow the Meltbead reference chart and source links embedded in each palette. Effect RGB values are approximations.
